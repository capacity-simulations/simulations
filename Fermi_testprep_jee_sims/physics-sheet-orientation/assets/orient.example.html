A bob on a 1 m string, held out at an angle and launched sideways. Two sliders set the whole experiment; everything else on screen is there to tell you what happened.

1What you'll work out

  • Why a conical pendulum holds its angle at one launch speed — and what it does at every other speed.
  • Which part of the bob's angular momentum about the suspension point survives the motion, and which parts don't.
  • What the launch itself fixes for good, and what that forces the bob to do as the string swings.

JEE / NCERT Class 11 — circular motion, the conical pendulum, and angular momentum about a point.

2What this simulation shows

{{D0}}

The bob hangs from a fixed point on a light string of length L = 1 m and has mass m = 0.5 kg. You hold it out at an angle θ₀, then release it with a level, sideways speed v₀. From that instant nothing touches it but the string and gravity — the sim integrates the real spherical pendulum, so the path you see is the motion, not an animation of a formula.

The one thing to watch is the string angle. It can hold steady, swing inwards, or swing outwards, and which of those happens is decided entirely by the two numbers you set before releasing.
