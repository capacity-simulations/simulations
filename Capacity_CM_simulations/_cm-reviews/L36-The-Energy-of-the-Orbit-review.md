# The Energy of the Orbit
**Lecture 36 · L36-The Energy of the Orbit.html**

**System:** Single-slider exploration of specific orbital energy in natural units (GM = m = 1). E runs −1.0 → +0.6; the sim draws the orbit alongside a V(r) = −GM m/r energy diagram with an E-line, a KE gap and a turning-radius marker, and steps the student through bound → parabolic → unbound via a 4-card guided inquiry.
**Verdict:** The **bound** regime is excellent — the propagation is analytic (Kepler's equation, no integrator), so ε, vis-viva and the virial relation are exact to ~1e-5 % with zero secular drift and no framerate dependence. But the sim has **two physics P0s**: the parabolic E = 0 branch uses the wrong time law and makes the body *accelerate* outward (falsifying its own escape-velocity equation), and the energy diagram plots V(r) while the orbit has L ≠ 0, so the marked "turning radius" is 2a while the body actually turns at 1.4a — the demonstration the inquiry text promises never happens. Two non-physics P0s on top: the sim **ships with Play disabled**, and Reset breaks the prediction gate on every step.
**Browser-probe:** Bundled sweep ran (5 states, 1 slider, `errors: []`) but cannot validate this sim (no `window.__audit`). Two parallel reviewers drove it directly — one instrumented a *copy* to expose `state`/`advance` (original untouched, md5 `2604b91a…` verified unchanged start to finish), one walked the inquiry, offline path, resets and three viewport widths. All four headline findings re-verified against source by me.

## PHYSICS
### P0
- [high] **The parabolic (E = 0) branch uses a linear-in-time Barker parameter, so the body speeds up as it escapes.** `advance()` L783: `state.anom += dt*0.6` treats D as linear in t. Barker's equation is `M = D + D³/3` with `M = √(GM/2q³)(t−τ)`, i.e. `dD/dt ∝ 1/(1+D²)`. Since `r = q(1+D²)`, a linear D gives `r ∝ t²` and a monotonically *increasing* speed — the exact opposite of a parabolic escape, where `v = √(2GM/r) → 0`. Measured at r = 16.2: on-screen speed **4.831 vs v_esc = 0.351 (+1276 %)**, implying ε = +11.6 rather than 0. This directly falsifies the sim's own `eq4: v_esc = √(2GM/r)` and the whole "E = 0 is the escape boundary" story. The conic *shape* (`r = q(1+D²)`, `ν = 2 atan D`) is correct — only the timing is wrong. → **Fix:** accumulate a Barker mean anomaly `state.anom += kp*dt` with `kp = Math.sqrt(GM/(2*q**3))`, then invert in closed form: `z = Math.cbrt(1.5*M + Math.sqrt(1+2.25*M*M)); D = z - 1/z`. No iteration needed. Freeze on `r` (e.g. `r > 18`) rather than on D. [evidence: `agentA/E-000-parabolic.png`]
- [high] **The energy diagram plots V(r), but the orbit has L ≠ 0 — so the marked turning radius is wrong and the KE bar never reaches zero.** `drawEnergyPanel()` L1037 marks `rTurn = −GM·m/E`, which with GM = m = 1 is exactly **2a** — the turning point for *radial infall* (L = 0). The plotted orbit has a fixed eccentricity `E_BOUND = 0.4` (`deriveConic()` L662), so it actually turns at `a(1+e) = 1.4a`. Measured ratio `r_turn / r_aphelion = 1.42857 = 2/1.4` at **every** E. The body therefore stops 30 % short of the dashed marker, permanently, with KE ≈ 0.214 still showing at E = −0.5 — while the Step-2/Step-4 feedback text asserts "the intersection is where KE = E − V(r) = 0 … so the body turns around". This is the classic V vs V_eff conflation: with L ≠ 0 the turning points are set by `V_eff = −GM m/r + L²/2mr²`. → **Fix (pick one):** (a) plot `V_eff` — L is fixed by a and e via `L² = GM m² a(1−e²)` — so the intersection genuinely *is* aphelion and KE → 0 there; or (b) keep V(r), relabel the marker `r_max (radial fall, L = 0)`, add a second marker at `a(1+e)` for the real aphelion, and rewrite the Step-2/Step-4 text. (a) is the honest fix for a sim whose lesson is "where does the body turn around". [evidence: `agentA/E-050-bound.png`]

### P1
- [high] **The body marker and KE bar are clamped to r ≤ 5, so the displayed KE is grossly inflated for |E| ≲ 0.14.** `drawEnergyPanel()` L1059: `const rNow = Math.max(rMin, Math.min(rMax, state.r)); const Vnow = -GM*m/rNow;` — the KE bar is drawn from `Y(Vnow)` to `Y(E)`, so clamping r inflates the gap. Measured at aphelion: E = −0.12 → 0.0800 drawn vs 0.0514 true (+55.6 %); **E = −0.10 → 0.100 vs 0.0429 (+133 %); E = −0.05 → 0.150 vs 0.0214 (+600 %)**. The marker also pins to the right border and the "KE" label spills outside the plot. → **Fix:** auto-scale `rMax` per regime (`rMax = Math.max(5, 1.15*(state.E<0 ? -GM*m/state.E : 5))`) instead of clamping, or hide the marker with a "body beyond plotted range" note when `state.r > rMax`. [evidence: `agentA/E-005-bound-near-zero.png`, `agentA/E-010-bound-offscreen.png`]
- [med] **The three regimes run on three different clocks**, so the comparison the slider is built for is contaminated. Bound advances at the true mean motion (`n·dt`, factor 1.000 — verified); unbound is scaled by **0.7** (`advance()` L764: `state.anom += n*dt*0.7`); parabolic by **0.6** (L783). Measured on hyperbolas: speed / vis-viva = **0.7000 exactly** at every r for both E = +0.2 and +0.6. The trajectory is right and the drawn KE bar (computed as E − V, not from v) is right, but a student reading speed off the animation gets KE = 0.49·KE_true on a hyperbola — and the apparent tempo jumps discontinuously as E crosses 0. → **Fix:** drop the 0.7 (and fix the parabolic branch per P0-a); pace with a shell speed control instead if the raw rate is too fast.

### P2
- [high] **Over the last fifth of the bound range the orbit is entirely outside the viewport.** `drawOrbitPanel()` L838 fixes `rangeVis = 4.0`, while aphelion = 0.7/|E| exceeds 4 for **|E| < 0.175** — so ~175 of the 1000 slider positions show no body at all. Worse, the `r_max` dashed marker is gated on `rTurn ≤ 5` (L1038) and vanishes for **|E| < 0.2** — i.e. the "bound ⇒ finite turning radius" evidence disappears exactly where the student is asked to approach E → 0⁻. On the unbound side, time-to-freeze is 96 s at E = +0.1 and 3031 s at E = +0.01, so the near-zero hyperbola is visually frozen off-screen. → **Fix:** make `rangeVis` adaptive (`clamp(1.3*state.rmax, 2, 20)` for bound) and scale the unbound anomaly rate so freeze time is regime-independent.
- [high] **`fmtE()` L1134 returns a leading space at E = 0**, so the parabolic readout renders as `" 0.00"`, while the canvas E-line label (L1033) uses `state.E>=0?'+':'−'` and shows `+0.00` at the same setting. → **Fix:** one formatter with a dead-band that prints unsigned `0.00`.

## Browser-test evidence
- **Bound-regime physics is exact.** Propagation is analytic (`advance()` L743: Kepler's equation by Newton, 8 iterations bound / 12 hyperbolic) — there is **no integrator**, so there is nothing to drift. `a` from the slider matches `−GM/2E` to 0.00 % at E = −1.0 / −0.5 / −0.15 / −0.1; live `ε = v²/2 − GM/r` matches the slider to <1e-4 % at five phases per orbit.
- **Vis-viva** at perihelion, aphelion and ν ≈ 90°: max error **1.5e-5 %** (e.g. E = −1 perihelion 2.1602462 vs 2.1602459).
- **Conservation** over 200 orbits at E = −1: ε ∈ [−1.0000022, −0.9999978], spread 4.4e-6 with **zero secular trend**; r ∈ [0.30000000, 0.69999999] vs analytic [0.3, 0.7].
- **Framerate independence**: identical x, y to 1e-9 for dt = 1e-4 / 1e-3 / 1e-2 / 0.05 at t = 10. There is **no `#shell-speed` control** in this sim (removed per the POLISH note L268; `speed` hard-coded to 1 at L466), so the fixed-substep failure mode of the sibling sims cannot occur.
- **Virial**, time-averaged over one orbit at E = −0.5: ⟨KE⟩ = 0.50000, ⟨PE⟩ = −1.00000, ⟨KE⟩/(−⟨PE⟩/2) = **1.00000**, sum = −0.50000 = E. PE is correctly negative and → 0⁻ at large r; the KE bar references the true zero axis.
- **Regime labelling** is correct across the sweep: −1.0…−0.001 → "bound"; exactly 0.000 → "parabolic"; +0.001…+0.6 → "unbound"; "no turning radius" appears only for E > 0.
- **Singularity guards** are sound: r never below 0.15; the V-plot floors at r = 0.15; Kepler denominators `|1−e cos E| ≥ 0.6` and `|e cosh F − 1| ≥ 0.3`; no page errors in any run.
- **Answer key is fully correct** on all four questions (V → 0 at large r; E < 0 ⇒ closed ellipse; E > 0 ⇒ hyperbola; past r_max, V > E would need negative KE).
- **Zero console errors** across load, play, reset, an 11-point slider sweep including exactly E = 0, theme toggle, Info, Maximize, Formal, and all four inquiry steps, at 1440×900, 1280×800 and 1100×750. No horizontal overflow at any width.

## To verify (human)
- Decide between the two fixes for physics P0-b. Plotting `V_eff` makes the intersection genuinely be the turning point and rescues the inquiry text unchanged, but it changes the curve students see from the familiar −1/r shape to one with a centrifugal barrier — that is a pedagogical choice, not a bug fix.
- Whether the fixed `E_BOUND = 0.4` eccentricity is intended. Everything about the energy story would be cleaner at e = 0 (circular), where r_turn and aphelion coincide and the V(r) reading is exactly right; the fixed e = 0.4 is what creates the 1.43× discrepancy.
- Confirm the parabolic branch after the Barker fix: the body should visibly *decelerate* toward zero speed as it recedes, which is the whole point of E = 0.
- Whether Lecture mode should ungate Play for every sim in this family, or only here — the same lecture-default script is appended to all of them, but this is the only one where all cards are `data-gate`, which is what makes Play dead on load.
