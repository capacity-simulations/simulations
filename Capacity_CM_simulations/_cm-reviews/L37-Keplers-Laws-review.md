# Kepler's Laws
**Lecture 37 · L37-Kepler's Laws.html**

**System:** Three-mode Kepler sim in natural units (GM = 4π², a in AU, T in yr) — **K1** ellipse with the Sun at a focus (eccentricity slider), **K2** equal areas in equal times (two swept-area windows at perihelion/aphelion), **K3** T² ∝ a³ across up to 7 selectable planets with a live log–log plot. Five-step guided inquiry.
**Verdict:** The **model** is right — velocity Verlet on `a = −GM r̂/r²`, correct vis-viva seeding, and angular momentum conserved to machine precision, so Kepler 2 is exact by construction. But the **numerics and the presentation are not**: a fixed 6-substep budget makes the orbit framerate- and speed-dependent (at e = 0.85, 4× the orbit is destroyed), the curve drawn as "the ellipse" is an open integrated path rather than an analytic ellipse, and three separate on-screen labels state the wrong physics. Two physics P0, plus a non-physics P0 where the step-4 demo shows the opposite of the answer it teaches.
**Browser-probe:** Bundled sweep ran (5 states, 1 slider, `errors: []`) but cannot validate this sim (no `window.__audit`). Two parallel reviewers drove it directly: one hooked `drawOrbitCanvas` to record `(t,x,y,vx,vy)` per frame and re-ran the sim's own `stepVerlet` at its real step size; one walked the full inquiry, offline path and label rendering. All six headline findings re-verified against source by me before writing.

## PHYSICS
### P0
- [high] **Fixed 6 substeps per frame ⇒ the orbit depends on frame rate and speed multiplier.** `onFrame` L911 `const substeps=6;` with `h = dtSim/6`, regardless of how fast the planet is moving. Kepler orbits need `h` to shrink near perihelion (∝ r^{3/2}); a fixed budget cannot. Measured against the analytic orbit at 60 fps: e = 0.6 at 1× → a error +0.06 %; **e = 0.85 at 1× → +3.55 % in a, +5.34 % in T; e = 0.85 at 4× → +172.8 % in a, +349.6 % in T**, and with `dt` at the shell's 0.1 s clamp the orbit is destroyed entirely (+17 000 %). Confirmed on screen: e = 0.85 at 4× gave r_max = 2.16 AU vs 1.85 expected and a jagged trail leaving the canvas. Energy drift is *bounded* (correct symplectic behaviour, ΔE ≈ oscillation amplitude), but at e = 0.85 that bounded offset is 3.6 % of |E| — the student is simply shown the wrong ellipse. → **Fix:** size the step to the local orbital timescale, e.g. `const hmax = 0.01*Math.sqrt(r*r*r/GM); const n = Math.max(6, Math.min(4000, Math.ceil(dtSim/hmax)));`. Measured: 28 substeps/frame at e = 0.85, 4× gives a error 0.002 %, T error −0.65 % — more accurate *and* cheaper than a fixed 96. [evidence: `agentA/05-e085-speed4x.png`]
- [high] **The curve drawn as "the ellipse" is an open, numerically integrated path.** `sampleOrbit` L567 builds it by Verlet-stepping 720 times over one period rather than evaluating the conic. Measured closure gap between first and last point: 0.00016 AU at e = 0, 0.0104 at e = 0.6, **0.714 AU at e = 0.85**, where the drawn r_max is 1.921 vs 1.850 analytic (+3.84 %) and the curve visibly splits into a doubled teal arc. A Kepler's-**first**-law canvas that does not render a closed ellipse defeats its own purpose. → **Fix:** draw it analytically — `E = 2πk/N; x = a(cos E − e); y = a√(1−e²)·sin E`. Exact, closed, framerate-independent and cheaper. [evidence: `agentA/02-k1-e0p85.png`]

### P1
- [high] **The first swept-area window is a partial window, so the panel displays A₁/A₂ ≈ 0.46 — the opposite of Kepler 2 — exactly while step 3 asks the student to "Confirm A₁ ≈ A₂".** `accumulate` L941-952 starts summing the moment `inside` is true, with no check that the window was *entered* from outside; after `rebuildOrbit` the planet sits at perihelion, dead centre of the perihelion window. Measured at e = 0.6: first A₁ = 0.11634 = **46.3 %** of the expected 0.25133, while A₂ = 0.25119 (99.9 %). Self-corrects after ~1.5 yr, but recurs whenever K2 is entered with the planet already inside a window. → **Fix:** add a `primed` flag per window — set it on the first frame with `!inside`, and only accumulate when `primed && inside`. [evidence: `agentA/06-k2-first-window-artifact.png`]
- [high] **The T²–a³ guide line is mislabelled.** `drawTPlot` L880-886 draws `y = 2x` on log(a³) vs log(T²) axes and labels it **`slope 2 (T ∝ a²)`**. On those axes slope 2 means T² ∝ a⁶, i.e. **T ∝ a³**; T ∝ a² would be slope 4/3. The code comment at L877 repeats the error ("T ∝ a² → T² ∝ a⁴ → slope 2"), which is where the confusion came from — that's the slope on log T vs log a, not on these axes. The companion `slope 2/3 (T ∝ a)` line is correct. Found independently by both reviewers. → **Fix:** either draw `(4/3)*x` and keep the label, or keep the line and relabel it `slope 2 (T ∝ a³)`; fix the comment too.
- [med] **The dashed segment labelled `a` is 2a long.** `drawOrbitCanvas` L756: `A1 = W(a*(1-e),0)`, `A2 = W(-a-a*e,0)` spans perihelion → aphelion = the **major axis, 2a**, and L761 centres the label `a` on it. In a sim whose third law is `T² = a³`, mislabelling the defining length is a real hazard. → **Fix:** draw from the ellipse centre `(−a·e, 0)` to perihelion `(a(1−e), 0)` and keep the label `a`; or keep the span and label it `2a`.

### P2
- [med] **K3 perihelion-crossing detector is frame-quantised and can mistime.** L970 `if(phiB>0.85 && phiA<0.15)`. Mercury advances 0.138 phase per frame at 4× (0.83 if `dt` hits the clamp), so the ±0.15 gate is marginal. This is the source of the −0.8 % / −1.0 % low bias measured on Mercury/Venus. → **Fix:** detect on `phiA < phiB` and interpolate the crossing time: `t_cross = t + dtSim·(1−phiB)/((1−phiB)+phiA)`.

## NON-PHYSICS
### P0
- [pedagogy][high] **The step-4 "heavier planet" demo shows the opposite of the answer it teaches.** `state.planetHeavy` is seeded at perihelion once in `rebuildOrbit` L579, but only integrated while `showHeavyDemo` is true (`onFrame` L925-926), whereas `state.planet` integrates every frame. `onStep` L1203 flips the flag on at step 4 without re-seeding, so the two bodies are at unrelated phases — observed ~150° apart, permanently. The card asks "do they stay together, or does one lag?", the keyed-correct rebuttal says *"Watch: the two planets stay locked together"*, and the canvas shows them separated forever — reinforcing the exact misconception the step exists to kill. → **Fix:** re-seed co-located when the step opens: `state.planetHeavy = {...state.planet};` before setting `showHeavyDemo = true`. [evidence: `agentB/10-step4-twoplanets.png`, `agentB/21-step4-sep-*.png`]

### P1
- [functional][high] **Reset on step 5 dead-ends the card.** `onReset` L994-1012 unconditionally calls `applyMode('K1')` and clears every `data-ready`, but never re-runs `onStep(inqStep)`. After Reset on step 5 the card still says "Add at least two planets from the chip row" while `panel-k3` is `display:none` and `#inq-next` stays enabled with the answer wiped. Step 3 desyncs the same way. → **Fix:** end `onReset()` with `onStep(Shell.step)` + `Shell.stepUnready()` rather than forcing K1. [evidence: `agentB/23-reset-desync-on-step5.png`]
- [functional][high] **KaTeX failure leaves the Formal panel blank with no fallback.** `renderFormal` L1213-1241 runs once via a single `setTimeout(…,60)` (L1246) guarded by `typeof katex!=='undefined'`. With jsdelivr blocked, `#eq-newton/#eq-k1/#eq-k2/#eq-k3` all render empty — the panel shows the heading "K1 — THE ORBIT" with nothing under it. No infinite-retry risk here (unlike L30/L35), but also no recovery from a CDN slower than 60 ms, which is common. This sim needs no three.js, so the vendored copy is irrelevant. → **Fix:** bounded retry (~40 × 150 ms) then plain-Unicode fallback strings in each `#eq-*` div. [evidence: `agentB/19-offline-formal.png`]
- [ux][high] **Canvas label collisions in two modes.** (a) In K2 the A₁ readout box is drawn over the "K2 · EQUAL AREAS IN EQUAL TIMES" title, making the title unreadable — a regression from the changelog note at L235 that moved these boxes upward. (b) In K3 with all 7 chips added, "Mercury"/"Venus"/"Earth" overlap each other and merge with the "2 AU" ring label into "Mercury2 AU". → **Fix:** (a) offset the A₁/A₂ boxes ~28 px below the title band; (b) suppress labels below ~30 px projected radius or offset them radially outward. [evidence: `agentB/08-step3-areas-measured.png`, `agentB/22-k3-all7.png`]

## Browser-test evidence
- **Kepler 2 is exact.** `dA/dt = ½|r × v|` over a full orbit: spread (max−min)/mean = **0.00000 %** at e = 0, 0.3, 0.6 and 0.85, each equal to the analytic `h/2` (3.141593 / 2.996888 / 2.513274 / 1.654937) to double precision. Velocity Verlet conserves angular momentum exactly for a central force — ΔL ≈ 4e-12 % over 200 orbits. This is the sharpest available test and the sim passes it cleanly.
- **Kepler 1** by least-squares conic fit: the Sun sits at distance a·e from the ellipse centre to within 0.005 % (e = 0.3) and 0.026 % (e = 0.6); the empty focus is drawn correctly at −2ae. At e = 0.85 the fit degrades to +0.99 % — a consequence of the P0 integrator, not of a wrong model.
- **Kepler 3** across all 7 planets, measured perihelion-to-perihelion: T²/a³ = 0.9839 (Mercury) → 1.0011 (Jupiter) → 1.0001 (Saturn), spread 2 % across a 24 000× range in a³, dominated by frame-quantised crossing detection rather than the dynamics (pure-integrator T error for the same planets is ≤0.05 %). Displayed T²/a³ reads 0.994–0.996. **PASS.**
- **Conservation:** ΔE over 200 orbits = +0.00009 % (e=0.3), +0.055 % (e=0.6), +3.41 % (e=0.85) — in every case ≈ the oscillation amplitude, i.e. bounded with no secular leak, correct for a symplectic integrator.
- **Step-size sensitivity** (the P0): e=0.6 at 1× → +0.058 % in a; e=0.85 at 1× → +3.55 %; e=0.85 at 4× → +172.8 %; `dt` at the 0.1 s clamp → orbit destroyed.
- **Inquiry answer key is correct on all four questions** — perihelion fastest ✓, equal areas ✓, heavier planet same period ✓, T(5.2 AU) ≈ 12 yr ✓ (5.2^1.5 = 11.86). Keys are semantic (`eq`/`peri`/`aph`, `light`/`same`/`heavy`) so reorder-desync is structurally unlikely, and all rebuttals match their option text. The correct answer is never the third option — a judgment call, not a defect.
- **Gating verified**: every `data-gate` card disables `#inq-next` until answered; step 3's extra "Confirm A₁ ≈ A₂" requirement works; step 5 correctly blocks answering with <2 planets; "Change answer" re-locks Next; last step's Next reads "Finish".
- **Zero console errors** across load, full 5-step walkthrough, wrong-answer retry, play/pause, reset, speeds 0.25×/1×/2×/4×, the full e sweep 0 → 0.85, and adding all 7 planets.
- **Opens in Lecture mode** (L1252 appended script rAF-clicks `#shell-lecture`) — consistent with the other CM sims; flagged as a product decision, not a defect.
