The Physics

The Coriolis force · a complete revision sheet · ball on a turntable

The Coriolis force isn't a push. It's what a straight line looks like from a spinning room.

01

Where does the curve come from?

{{D1}}
One launch, two cameras — both paths computed from the same motion.
  • The room is an inertial frame: after launch no real sideways force acts, so the path is straight (Newton I).
  • The turntable is a non-inertial frame: ride it, and the very same flight looks bent.
  • Nothing touched the ball. Only the camera changed.
{{EQ1}}
Ground camera — real forces only.
{{EQ2}}
Table camera — two bookkeeping terms appear. Neither comes from any interaction.
02

Which way does it bend?

{{EQ6}}
  • Always perpendicular to the motion — it steers, never speeds up or slows down. Zero work; speed is untouched.
  • Ω > 0 (counterclockwise table): deflects to the right of the motion. Ω < 0: to the left.
  • No velocity in the rotating frame → no Coriolis force at all. Sitting still on the table you only feel mΩ²r, outward.
{{D2}}
03

How strong is the push?

{{EQ3}}
Ω v

These are the sim's real sliders — drag here and the turntable follows.

  • Double Ω or double v → the force doubles. Kill either → it vanishes.
04

So is the force even real?

{{EQ4}}
The bent path is the straight path re-plotted by a rotating camera — this is the exact transform the sim computes, point by point.
  • "Fictitious" doesn't mean useless. Inside the rotating frame the bookkeeping is exact — every prediction comes out right.
  • Step outside the frame and the force is simply gone. That is the entire answer to "is it real?".
05

Where does Earth keep its turntable?

{{EQ5}}
  • Ω⊕ = 7.29×10⁻⁵ rad/s — per kilogram, about 137× weaker than this tabletop. Bending a path takes hours and hundreds of kilometres.
  • North: deflects right → air falling into a low spins counterclockwise. South: left → clockwise.
  • At the equator the horizontal effect vanishes — tropical cyclones never form there.
  • Bathtub myth: at sink scale it's ~10⁻⁷ of gravity. The real fingerprints are jet streams, ocean gyres, long-range artillery, and Foucault's pendulum.
{{D3}}
06

The traps they'll set for you

  • "Coriolis is a real push." No — it's the price of a rotating frame. In the ground frame it doesn't exist.
  • "It acts on everything on the table." Only on things moving in the rotating frame — F = 2mΩv needs a v.
  • "It speeds the ball up." Never — perpendicular to the motion, so it changes direction only.
  • "Drains spin backwards in Australia." Orders of magnitude too weak at sink scale.
07

Ninety-second self-test

A ball rests on the spinning table. What Coriolis force does it feel?

Zero — F = 2mΩv needs velocity in the rotating frame. It still feels the centrifugal term mΩ²r, pointing outward.

Double Ω and halve v. What happens to the force?

Nothing — it depends on the product Ωv, so 2Ω × v/2 leaves it unchanged. (The path still differs: flight time and frame rotation change.)

Why does the ground camera never see the curve?

Because no real sideways force exists — the curve is manufactured by rotating coordinates: rrot = R(−θ)·rlab. Straight line in, curved re-plot out.
