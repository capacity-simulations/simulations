About: Wire and Solenoid — One Field Thins Out, the Other Does Not

Source

Topic
Magnetic field of a long straight wire and a long solenoid (Ampère's law)
Concept
The straight wire's field falls as 1/r; a long solenoid's interior field is uniform, set only by n and I.
Audience
JEE / NCERT Class 11–12 — Moving Charges & Magnetism
Inquiry
Predict, then observe: doubling the distance from the wire HALVES B; moving inside the solenoid does not change B at all.
Misconception
"Magnetic fields fall off as 1/r², like Coulomb's law." — false for a straight wire (1/r) and irrelevant inside a long solenoid (independent of position).

Guided Inquiry

1 · Look at the two fields

Left: a long straight wire, seen end-on. Its field wraps around it as circles that thin out with distance. Right: a long solenoid, cutaway. Inside, the arrows are evenly spaced. Same current in both.

Take it in, then hit Next.

2 · Predict — the wire

The probe near the wire currently reads some value B₀. You now move it to twice the distance from the wire. What does the reading become?

3 · Predict — the solenoid

The probe inside the solenoid reads some value. You move it to twice its distance from the solenoid's central axis (still inside). What does the reading become?

4 · Now drive it

Drag the yellow probe on the LEFT away from the wire — watch the reading halve as the distance doubles. Drag the probe on the RIGHT anywhere inside the solenoid — the reading refuses to move. Then step outside the windings: it collapses to nearly zero.

Change the current (shared) and the solenoid's n (turns/m). Read off the numbers: they will match B = μ₀I/(2πr) and B = μ₀ n I exactly.

Long straight wire

Ampère's law on a circle of radius r around the wire. Falls as 1/r — not 1/r².

Long solenoid, inside

Ampère's law on a rectangular loop straddling the winding. Depends only on n (turns/m) and I — not on where you stand or the solenoid's radius.

Force on 1 m of wire carrying I, inside the solenoid

If the wire crosses the axis (θ = π/2), the force per metre is BI. This is the force reported by the audit hook.

μ₀

Permeability of free space — CODATA value used inside the engine.
