Guided Inquiry

1 · Predict

You will drag the probe ★ from close to the charge out to far away. As you do, both the potential curve V(r) and the field strength E change. What happens?

2 · Observe

Drag the probe ★ on the left panel. Watch the tangent line on the right — its slope IS the gradient dV/dr. Notice that the two readouts E and −dV/dr agree to four decimal places at every position, every charge.

Move the probe close: contours crowd, V(r) plunges, tangent line stands up steep, both numbers climb together. Move it far: contours space out, V(r) flattens, tangent line lies almost flat, both numbers shrink together.

3 · Verify

Look at the ratio (−dV/dr) / E in the sidebar as you drag. What value does it hold?

4 · Resolve

The map contains the field. A contour map of V is not a separate diagram from a field map — it IS the field map. Where the contours crowd, V changes rapidly with distance; that rate of change, negated, is E.

The units differ (V vs V/m) precisely because one is the rate of change of the other with distance. Explore freely: change q, drag the probe anywhere, and confirm — the two numbers are always equal.
