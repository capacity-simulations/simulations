Guided Inquiry

1. Fire straight up, just below escape

The launch speed is set to 10.5 km/s from Earth's surface — just under escape speed. The angle is 90° (straight up). Press ▶ Play and watch the projectile. Does it leave and never return, or come back?

2. Predict — same speed, tilted to 45°

Keep the launch speed at 10.5 km/s, but tilt the launcher to 45°. Does the projectile now escape?

3. Watch the 45° launch

The launcher is now at 45°. Press ▶ Play. Watch the path — and the energy bar, now on the right. Does the sign of E change when only the direction changes?

4. Predict — a marble vs a spaceship

A 1 g marble and a 1000 t spaceship both launch straight up from Earth. Which needs the higher launch speed to escape?

5. Cross the threshold

The speed slider and readouts are now live. Push v₀ up through the vesc mark on the slider and watch KE + PE. Where does the total-energy sign flip, and does the path stop coming back?

Then try the Moon and Jupiter. Does vesc / vcirc stay √2 on every world?
