Guided Inquiry

1 · A loop is already on the mat

The demo is a single closed loop: battery, bulb, and a closed switch. Extra parts sit on the rack behind the table — drag one onto the mat to add it.

Press Next, then predict what the currents do at a junction.

2 · Predict — current at a node

This loop is series — one path. At the node just after the battery, how do the currents compare?

3 · Show the charges

Tick Show current in the right panel. Watch the moving charges on every segment of the loop.

Are the three wires carrying different currents, or the same?

4 · KCL in one sentence

KCL: the algebraic sum of currents at a node is zero. In a single loop there is nowhere to branch, so every segment carries the same I. A junction is not a sink.

5 · Predict — around the loop

The battery is 9 V. Wires here are almost resistanceless. Walking once around the loop, the bulb's voltage drop must be…

6 · Two conservation laws

KCL conserves charge at every node; KVL conserves energy around every loop. Open the switch: I goes to zero and both laws still hold (0 = 0).
