About the Simulation Physics: A 2D Gaussian wavepacket evolves under free-particle spreading (ℏ = m = 1). The probability density |ψ(x,y,t)|² is normalized on the full plane, so ∫∫|ψ|² dx dy = 1 at all times. Pressing Measure performs a position measurement: the outcome is sampled exactly from |ψ|² within the finite viewing window (renormalized there by the Born rule), and the state collapses to a narrow Gaussian centered at the measured point, approximating a position eigenstate. The post-collapse packet then re-spreads according to the uncertainty principle.

Visuals: In 3D mode, surface height is proportional to |ψ|² (clamped when the peak exceeds the display range; the actual peak is shown in the statistics panel). In Heatmap mode, color maps to |ψ|² on a fixed scale with a legend. The plotted square is finite, so the visible probability can be slightly below 1 when tails extend beyond the view.

Controls: Use Play/Reset, switch between 3D and Heatmap views, and press Measure to trigger collapse. Sliders set initial position, measurement width, and animation speed. The initial packet uses the same σ as the measurement collapse. All quantities are in natural units (ℏ = m = 1).

Guided Inquiry

1 · The packet

A single particle moves freely in two dimensions. The surface is |ψ(x, y, t)|², the probability density for finding it at (x, y), normalised so that ∫∫|ψ|² dx dy = 1. Left alone the packet spreads; a measurement of position returns one point.

2 · One measurement

Classically the particle already sits at one definite point, and the hump only records our ignorance of which. Let the packet spread, then measure its position. What is returned?

3 · Core and surround

The colour map shows |ψ|² directly: a bright core fading into a dark surround where |ψ|² is small but never zero. Over many measurements, how do the outcomes divide between core and surround?

4 · Immediately after an outcome

A broad packet is measured and returns a point. Without letting time pass, the position is measured again. Where does the second outcome fall?
