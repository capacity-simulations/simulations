About this simulation

Physics: The Stern-Gerlach experiment measures spin along a chosen axis. Z-magnets split atoms into Z+ and Z−, while X-magnets split into X+ and X−. Switching measurement axis changes the state basis, so prior spin information along a different axis is no longer definite.

Visuals: The 3D view shows the atom source, magnet stages, optional blockers, beam branching, and detector hits. Beam direction and color encode the current spin channel, and the legend maps colors to unpolarized, Z, and X outcomes.

Controls: Build Stage 2/3 by dragging magnets or blockers, set Stage 1 orientation/blocker, tune emission rate and field strength, and use play/pause, reset, and stats controls. Turn on Explore to see hover explanations on apparatus parts.

Guided Inquiry

1 · Sequential stages

In this experiment we consider sequential Stern–Gerlach stages, instead of just a single stage. A Z magnet measures the spin component along z and an X magnet measures it along x; each deflects a beam according to the value it measures. A blocker placed after a magnet stops one of that magnet’s two output beams.

2 · A second magnet

An X magnet now stands after the first Z magnet. How many regions of the detector screen are lit up?

3 · One output blocked

The spin-down (↓) output of the first Z magnet is blocked, and a Z magnet occupies the second slot. How many regions of the detector screen are lit up?

4 · A third stage

The ↓ output of the first Z magnet is still blocked. An X magnet follows, with its ← output blocked. A Z magnet fills the third slot. How many regions of the detector screen are lit up?
