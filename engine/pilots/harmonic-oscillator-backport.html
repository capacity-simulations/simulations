About this simulation

This simulation models a 1D quantum harmonic oscillator in units where ℏ = 1, with user-controlled mass m and angular frequency ω.

  • Harmonic potential: the parabolic potential V(x) = ½ mω²x² with the energy levels En = (n + ½)ℏω; the selected level is drawn between its classical turning points.
  • Wavefunction: the real and imaginary parts of the time-dependent eigenstate ψ(x,t) = ψn(x) e−iEnt/ℏ.
  • Probability density: |ψ(x,t)|², which is stationary for an energy eigenstate.

Use the sidebar to change the quantum state n (now 0–30 — the engine's stable Hermite recurrence stays accurate at high n), the mass m, and the angular frequency ω, and watch the eigenstate width and the energy spacing change. The speed control only changes how fast the plotted phase advances on screen.
